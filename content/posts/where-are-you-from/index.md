So, where are you from?

A seemingly simple question that opens a floodgate of memories and emotions for me.

Let me paint you a picture of my journey. Born to Kannada-speaking parents in Belgaum, Karnataka, my childhood unfolded in the vibrant streets and sandy-beaches of Goa, where my father's teaching career blossomed. From there, life took me to Jalandhar for engineering studies, then to the serene landscapes of Thiruvananthapuram for training, and eventually, the bustling streets of Mumbai for work. With marriage, Bengaluru became home, while my parents retraced their steps back to Belgaum post-retirement.

"So, where are you from?" - This question once perplexed me. Do I say Goa, where my childhood echoes but no longer holds a place to call home? Belgaum, where the language flows naturally but emotions remain detached? Jalandhar, the land of fun and friendships yet tinged with pain? Mumbai, where financial independence bloomed but came with heartbreak and bankruptcy? Or Bengaluru, where family and roots now anchor me?

Perhaps there is no 'right' answer. Over time, I've turned the tables with a playful, "Let's see if you can guess?" Finding amusement in their responses and reasoning has become my new norm.
